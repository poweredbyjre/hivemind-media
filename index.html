What I do

Short-form edits

Vertical videos cut for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Tight pacing, clean cuts and a first second that gives people a reason to stay.

Long-form edits

YouTube-style videos up to 15 minutes. I trim the dead air, shape the story and keep the pace moving from intro to outro.

Clips from long-form

One long video becomes a batch of short clips. You record once and still have something to post for the rest of the month.

Captions & thumbnails

Captions that are easy to read with the sound off. Thumbnails are available as an add-on when you need the full package.
